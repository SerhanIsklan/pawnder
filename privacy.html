Privacy Policy
PrivacyEffective 17 October 2026
The short version: we collect nothing ourselves. No account, no analytics. Your games, progress and settings stay on your phone. The ads come from Google AdMob, which uses your advertising ID; REMOVE ADS turns them off.

1Who we are

Pawnder is made by Nevermiss Studio ("we"). Questions about this policy: serhanisiklan@gmail.com.

2What we collect

We collect nothing ourselves. The app has no servers of ours and no accounts. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and it has no analytics or crash reporting. It does include two Google services, described below: AdMob, which shows the ads, and Google Play Billing, which handles the REMOVE ADS purchase.

3What stays on your phone

Everything the app remembers is kept in its private storage on your device, where only the app can read it:

  • your games and their reviews, puzzle results and your mistakes saved as puzzles
  • LEARN progress, stars, your rating, streak and weekly ladder
  • your settings, and the first name you can type in the intro (optional)

We never see any of it, and none of it is shared with the ads.

4Ads (Google AdMob)

Until you buy REMOVE ADS, the app shows an ad between some games, and ads you can choose to watch: to keep a streak, or to replay a lost ladder game the same day. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software collects your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which gives a rough location), device and app details such as model, Android version and language, and how you interact with the ads. Google handles this under Google's Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where the law asks for it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

5Purchases (Google Play)

REMOVE ADS is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns from Google Play whether the purchase was made, so it can turn the ads off.

6Internet

The game itself works fully offline. The app goes online only to load ads and to check the REMOVE ADS purchase with Google Play. Without a connection everything still works; you just see no ads.

7Backups

If backup is switched on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data to your Google account so it comes back on a new phone. Google handles that copy under Google's Privacy Policy; we never receive it. You can switch backup off in Android settings.

8Permissions

Internet and network state (for ads and Google Play), the advertising ID (for ads), billing (for REMOVE ADS), and vibration (a light tick while the intro text types; turn it off in Settings > Haptics).

9Children

The app is meant for players aged 13 and over. It has no chat, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

10Other companies

Google, for the ads and Google Play, as described above. The chess engines in the app (Stockfish and Maia) run on your phone and send nothing anywhere.

11Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR and Türkiye's KVKK give you rights over personal data held about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. For the data Google uses for ads, use Settings > Ad privacy in the app, your Android ad settings, or Google's My Ad Center. To delete everything the app stored, uninstall it or clear its storage in Android settings.

12Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.
