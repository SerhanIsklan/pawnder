Privacy Policy
PrivacyEffective 16 October 2026
The short version: we collect nothing. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your games, progress and settings stay on your phone.

1Who we are

Pawnder is made by Nevermiss Studio ("we"). Questions about this policy: serhanisiklan@gmail.com.

2What we collect

Nothing. The app has no servers and no accounts. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and it does not use advertising IDs, analytics or crash-reporting services.

3What stays on your phone

Everything the app remembers is kept in its private storage on your device, where only the app can read it:

  • your games and their reviews, puzzle results and your mistakes saved as puzzles
  • LEARN progress, stars, your rating, streak and weekly ladder
  • your settings, and the first name you can type in the intro (optional)

We never see any of it.

4No internet

The app works fully offline. The Android app does not even ask for permission to use the internet, so nothing can be sent from it.

5Backups

If backup is switched on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data to your Google account so it comes back on a new phone. Google handles that copy under Google's Privacy Policy; we never receive it. You can switch backup off in Android settings.

6Permissions

Vibration only, for a light tick while the intro text types. You can turn it off in Settings > Haptics.

7Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. It has no ads, no chat and no purchases.

8Other companies

None. The chess engines in the app (Stockfish and Maia) run on your phone and send nothing anywhere.

9Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR and Türkiye's KVKK give you rights over personal data held about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. To delete everything the app stored, uninstall it or clear its storage in Android settings.

10Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. If the app ever needs to collect anything, it will ask you first, inside the app.
